description: The Monday review written from the attention list, the week's meetings, follow-ups and the calendar
---

Run `attention`, `upcoming`, `follow-ups`, `notes-due`, `calendar` and `blackouts` with `--json`. Write `drafts/weekly-review-YYYY-MM-DD.md`: meetings this week and anything wrong with them, notes and promises owed, deadlines and blackouts in the next 30 days, holders to call. Nothing sends.

Ambiguous names list the candidates and stop: ask which one. Never send anything; drafts stay in `drafts/`.
