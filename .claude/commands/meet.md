---
description: Book or record a meeting with an investor, refusing a blackout or unreleased material unless the disclosure officer says yes
---

Work out the institution, date, kind, our team and their people from the ask, then run `npm run ir -- meet <institution> --date=YYYY-MM-DD --kind=.. --team=HM,RP --contacts=.. [--start=HH:MM] [--roadshow=..] [--material=..]`. A past date records it as held; a future date books it. If it refuses for a blackout or unreleased material, say why and stop. Only add `--force` when the operator says the disclosure officer has agreed, in this session.

Ambiguous names list the candidates and stop: ask which one. Never send anything; drafts stay in `drafts/`.
