description: The market calendar: results, quarterlies, the AGM and investor days, with each deadline and whether it is lodged

Run `npm run ir -- calendar --json`. Lead with anything due in the next 30 days and anything planned after its deadline. Record a lodgement with `/lodge`.

Ambiguous names list the candidates and stop: ask which one. Never send anything; drafts stay in `drafts/`.
